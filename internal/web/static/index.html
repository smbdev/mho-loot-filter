Item search

Find any item and choose what to hide, or hear, when it drops.

Item groups

Hide or hear whole kinds of loot at once.

Hero uniques

Hide or hear one hero's own Unique items.

Every hero has their own Unique items, shown in game as "Unique - Rogue" and so on. Use All heroes to switch every hero's uniques at once, or pick a hero to switch all of theirs, then switch single ones back, for example the one you are farming. Uniques any hero can use are in the Uniques group under Item groups.

Rarity

Switch off rarity glows, hear Cosmic and Unique drops, or hide items by rarity.

Regular gear, insignias, relics and team-up gear glow in the colour of their rarity when they drop. Turn a colour off to remove that glow from every one of those items with that rarity. The items themselves stay visible.

When one of those items drops as Cosmic or Unique, it plays that rarity's sound instead of its own, so its Play sound switch in Item search is not heard. Switch on Play sound here to hear every Cosmic or every Unique drop.

Uniques, artifacts, Fortune Cards and chests have their own glow instead. Switch that off from Item search, or for every item at once with Every glow.

Hide by rarity

Hide a kind of item only when it drops at the rarities you tick, for example every medallion below Cosmic. Those drops have no model, glow or name, and cannot be clicked, so you never pick them up by accident.

Alert sound

What you hear when an item with Play sound drops.

Play uses this volume, so you can try it before applying.

Any common sound file works, such as MP3, WAV, OGG or FLAC. Quiet sounds are raised to full volume, and anything after the first 10 seconds is cut. In game, the alert follows the Sound Effects Volume setting.

A new sound or volume is used the next time you click Apply to game. Each profile has its own.

My filter

Everything you have chosen to hide or hear.

Each profile keeps its own filter and alert sound, for example one for Holo-Sim and one for everything else. Pick a profile in the menu on the left, then click Apply to game.

Share your filter with a friend: export it to a file, and they import it here.

Game folder

Where Marvel Heroes Omega is installed.

The folder that contains UnrealEngine3 and Data.

Game tweaks

Game settings that are not in the game's Options.

Tweaks are used the next time you click Apply to game and start the game. They are the same for every profile. Restore all game files puts the game's own settings back.

Mute sounds

Silence game sounds you are tired of hearing.

Single sounds

Sounds are named after the game's own sound files. Muted sounds are the same for every profile and are used the next time you click Apply to game. Restore all game files unmutes everything.

Backups and restore

Put the game back exactly as it was.

Before a game file is changed for the first time, the original is copied to %LOCALAPPDATA%\MHOLootFilter.

Restoring puts every changed game file back to its original and clears the filter of the profile in use, the game tweaks and the muted sounds.

After Steam "Verify integrity of game files", click Apply to game again.
